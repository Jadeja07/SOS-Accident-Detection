Smart Accident Detection & Emergency Alert System

A next-generation IoT-based SOS system that automatically detects accidents, tracks location, records video, and alerts family & emergency services.

Powered By

  • 📞 SIM900A – Call & SMS Alert
  • 📍 NEO-6M GPS – Speed & Location
  • 🧠 MPU6050 – Accident Detection

Key Features

Everything needed to save lives in critical situations

🚨 Automatic Accident Detection

Using MPU6050 sensor, the system detects sudden impact, tilt, and abnormal motion instantly.

📍 Live Location Tracking

NEO-6M GPS sends accurate location and speed to emergency contacts and server.

📞 Smart Call & SMS Alert

SIM900A automatically sends SMS and calls emergency contacts without human interaction.

☁️ Cloud & Dashboard Ready

Integrated with Firebase backend for monitoring and history tracking.

How It Works

Simple, fast, and fully automatic

1️⃣ Accident occurs → MPU6050 detects abnormal motion & impact.
2️⃣ GPS module fetches real-time location & speed.
3️⃣ SIM900A sends SMS and makes calls to emergency contacts.
4️⃣ Data is stored and viewed on the web dashboard.

Built to Save Lives

Designed for bikes, cars, and public transport to reduce response time and increase survival chances.
